Welcome to SyncTool

This will install SyncTool on your Mac — a lightweight file sync tool you can control from your browser.

What gets installed:

  • SyncTool API server port 3001
  • SyncTool Agent (runs your sync jobs)
  • synctool command-line utility

Both services start automatically when you log in and run entirely on your machine. No data leaves your computer.

After installation, open http://localhost:3001 to configure your first sync job.
